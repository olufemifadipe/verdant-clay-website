VERDANT CLAY KY — WEBSITE PREVIEW

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract this ZIP first (Windows: right-click > Extract All).
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox.
3. Keep the files and assets folder together.

FILES
index.html — page content
styles.css — design and responsive layout
app.js — collection filtering and product details
favicon.svg — browser icon
assets/collection.png — supplied product imagery

The site works without a build step. An internet connection loads the optional Google fonts and opens Instagram/WhatsApp links. WhatsApp buttons prepare enquiries; visitors choose whether to send them.

FOR THE BUSINESS OWNER
This is a design preview. Product photos were taken from the screenshots provided; original high-resolution photos would improve the final site. Please confirm product names, descriptions, WhatsApp details, and availability before a public launch. Prices and checkout are not included.

HOSTING
Upload the website files, keeping the folder structure intact, to any static website host. index.html should be at the site root. No ChatGPT login or paid framework is required to open these exported files.
